In August 2026, I attended the 8th International Conference on Environmental Dimensions of Antibiotic Resistance (EDAR8) in Brisbane, Australia. I delivered an oral presentation titled “MobiGLM: An Augmented Genomic Language Model Enabling Mobility-Tiered Risk Stratification for Antibiotic Resistance Genes.” It was a valuable opportunity to share my research on artificial intelligence-enabled antimicrobial resistance risk assessment, receive constructive feedback, and exchange ideas with researchers from around the world.
